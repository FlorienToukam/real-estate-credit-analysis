# Simon Property Group credit analysis

I reviewed Simon Property Group's operating performance, liquidity and debt exposure, then tested how higher interest rates and weaker property income could affect debt service and refinancing.

The workbook has two parts. The issuer analysis uses published Simon disclosures. A separate illustrative property case compares the risks borne by senior debt, junior debt and common equity.

## Start here

1. Read the [two-page credit memorandum](Simon_Property_Group_Credit_Memorandum.pdf).
2. Open the [Excel model](Simon_Property_Group_Credit_Model.xlsx).
3. Use `Historical` for company financials, `Issuer Stress` for the rate test and `Property Case` for the illustrative financing comparison. `Sources` records the references and scope.

## Main findings

- A 200-basis-point rate increase adds $37.1 million of annual run-rate interest on Simon's reported economic share of variable-rate debt at June 2026.
- A separate $2.0 billion fixed-debt refinancing scenario adds $40.0 million of interest. That amount is an analyst-selected scenario, not a reported maturity allocation.
- The illustrative property's combined downside lowers senior DSCR from 1.43x to 0.89x and raises LTV from 60.0% to 93.8%.
- A 65% refinancing LTV limit allows $41.6 million of replacement senior debt against $60 million outstanding, creating an $18.4 million funding gap.

My conclusion was to require lower leverage or a credible equity funding plan in the property downside. A value sufficient to repay senior principal at sale does not solve an interim cash shortfall or refinancing gap.

## Model assumptions

The illustrative property starts at $100 million value and $6 million NOI, financed with $60 million senior debt, $10 million junior debt and $30 million common equity. The downside reduces NOI by 20%, increases the capitalization rate by 150 basis points and raises senior interest by 200 basis points.

Property value is NOI divided by the capitalization rate. DSCR, LTV and debt yield are calculated separately. Replacement debt is limited by the lowest amount allowed under the selected LTV, DSCR and debt-yield thresholds. The thresholds belong to this exercise.

The issuer's annualized NOI/interest calculation is an analyst proxy. It is not a reconstructed company covenant or cash-flow DSCR. The property case is not a Simon property appraisal or an actual financing transaction.
